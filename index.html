The context02

A bank partnership with no bureaucracy is already almost a mobile operator.

Tocrespo and GCB Bank together already have the customer, the accounts, the wallet and verified identity. The only missing layer is the network — and that is exactly the piece an MVNE delivers as a managed service.

🏦

What Tocrespo & GCB Bank already have

GCB Bank's account customers, verified KYC/identity, wallet and credit (GCB Advance) — paired with Tocrespo's brand, distribution and telecom go-to-market.

📶

What is missing

Spectrum, towers, core network, SIM/eSIM, telco billing, host-operator connection and licensing — the telco "machinery".

⚙️

What the MVNE delivers

All of that machinery, pre-integrated and operated as a managed service — the difference between a multi-year telco build and a managed go-live.

The logic04

Why an MVNO makes sense for Tocrespo and GCB Bank.

This is not diversification for its own sake. For Tocrespo and GCB Bank, mobile deepens exactly what the partnership promises customers: access, primacy and inclusion.

👑

Primacy

The mobile number is identity in Ghana. Owning it makes Tocrespo the first screen, every day.

🌍

Inclusion

Affordable data and voice reach the unbanked where credit and accounts have not yet arrived.

💳

Banking uplift

Airtime, wallet and credit on the same customer lift frequency, balances and retention.

📊

Data

Usage and top-up signals feed credit scoring and offers — in a governed way.

🔁

One customer, two revenue engines

Telco margin per subscriber is modest; the real value is the banking uplift — frequency, data and lower churn — which typically exceeds the direct telco margin.

🛡️

Asset-light, and yours

No spectrum, towers or RAN. A managed Full MVNO setup, phased with a decision gate at each step — and at the end the operator is Tocrespo's.

The engagement06

Four workstreams, one managed service.

The engagement runs as one integrated stream. Each workstream is a self-contained unit of work — Tocrespo can commission the full programme or the modules that matter most right now.

🧭

01 · Strategy & CVP

Segments and value propositions, launch offers, tariff roadmap, and the business case: subscribers, ARPU, CAC, churn, NPV and payback.

🔧

02 · Build & integration

BSS/OCS platform, billing, CRM and provisioning; SIM/eSIM; integration to the host operator and to GCB Bank's banking core (accounts, wallet, KYC).

🚀

03 · Launch

In-app onboarding, SIM/eSIM distribution, self-care (app/USSD), contact centre, number porting and go-to-market — from pilot to public launch.

📈

04 · Run & grow

End-to-end managed service: network operations, VAS, fraud, reporting, and continuous offer optimisation tied to banking signals.
